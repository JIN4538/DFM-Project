DMP Flex 350 Dual

Midsize dual laser metal 3D printer designed for application development, R&D, and production

Talk to an Expert

Two is Better Than One:

Our dual laser metal printer, the DMP Flex 350 Dual metal 3D printer takes the features and benefits of our single laser and makes it faster. Advanced features you’d expect such as bidirectional recoating, industry leading vacuum chamber for the lowest oxygen content, two strong 500 W lasers, glove port access and more! The standard build envelope is 275 x 275 x 420 mm and can swapped out for efficient material changeover making the DMP Flex 350 Dual a standout choice for increased application and material flexibility.

View Technical Specifications

Why DMP Flex 350 Dual?

  • Build Flexibility

    Swap out print modules with ease to maximize printing versatility.

  • Laser Productivity

    The DMP Flex 350 is a reliable choice for low to medium production environments. 

  • Vacuum Chamber

    Best-in-class vacuum chamber design maintains <25 ppm oxygen environment for the purest printing atmosphere resulting in superior surface finish. 

  • NoSupports

    NoSupports enables metal powder-bed printing without support structures which accelerates production and delivery.

Software

3DXpert: Industry leading software for Additive Manufacturing

Fully Integrated - Single Environment

3DXpert is your one-stop-shop for additive manufacturing. Streamline your workflow, maximize efficiency, and avoid costly errors, by completing all AM processes in the same software environment, including DfAM, build preparation, simulation, and inspection.

Achieve up to 75% faster file processing time with CAD to Slice workflows. No more compromising data integrity of mechanically designed parts to STLs. 3DXpert is built on a CAD engine - making history-based, parametric build preparation the new standard in AM. Hybrid DfAM combines the power of solid, mesh, and implicit (voxel-based) modeling.

3DXpert can help you handle any challenge AM throws at you. When faced with repetitive tasks, or part types that re-occur in your trays, 3DXpert can carry a lot of your load for you. By capturing your knowledge into scripts and using AI to group similar parts together and apply solutions to them, you are free to focus on more value-added tasks. Learn More

NoSupports

DMP Flex 350 Dual is NoSupports technology compatible. NoSupports enables metal powder-bed printing without support structures which accelerates production and delivery. Learn More

Accessories

Volume Reducer Module

This module allows you to maximize your investment through enabling you to print small research parts and also maintain production capabilities with the same printer. Requiring only one tenth of the powder, you no longer need to be restricted in experimenting with exotic alloys!

DMP Inspection 

DMP Inspection allows you to minimize secondary inspection with validated automated analysis. Take corrective action before you print! Our algorithms detect:

  • Lack of fusion
  • Warpage
  • Down-facing surface roughness
  • Recoating quality

DMP Monitoring 

DMP Monitoring is real-time process monitoring for informed decisions on product quality. Achieve higher quality output with synchronized side-by-side comparison of the meltpool and vision data. Learn more
